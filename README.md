The movie is about a really romantic couple who lived happily together, loved each other to the ends and had great chemistry. However their love story takes a dark twist when they reveal their darkest secrets.
The lead Charlie falls into this perplexity where he finds himself hard to accept Emma who was supposed to be the love of his life for who she really was. He wanted to uncover more of what happened to my senses it means
he was trying so hard to understand how Emma as a child could have thought of conducting a mass shooting in her school. He finds himself broken into two halves where one half wants to run away from her; the psychopath
while the other was trying so hard to reason, to stay. Emma on the other hand finds it so hard to open up about herself, she clearly had not fully apologized herself, it was hard watching her try to prove herself innocent
and not questioning her own integrity. Now being a viewer, i do resonate and feel empathetic towards Emma, as she proves to be a normal girl in a normal world, shes happy and has not really hurt people physically. Maybe
her childhood were shaped with wounds of a kind but in the end she did choose not to act on her impulses which overall convinces me the Charlie shouldnt have doubted her so much as after that one childhood incident she
never went in such a direction and never with him either.
One another beautiful perspective Emma brings to the movie with her character is that she seems super self composed. Even when things ruin she keeps coming back to it, trying to bring life with a newer perspective. I did enjoy
the movie, it felt fresh and new and short. so i rate it 8/10.
