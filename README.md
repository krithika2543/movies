The movie is about a deeply romantic couple who lived happily together, loved each other to the ends and had great chemistry. However their love story takes a unpleasant twist when they reveal their darkest secrets.
The lead Charlie falls into confusion where he finds hard to accept Emma, the love of his life for who she really was. He wanted to uncover more of what happened, which to my senses seems like his attempt to understand her. Emma as a growing teenager faced the scrutiny of bullyism and inability to fit in; had a really challenging childhood. It went to a level of severity where she had an intention of conducting a mass shooting in her school. Coming into such an awareness about Emma's past, Charlie finds himself broken into two halves where one half wants to run away from her; the psychopath
while the other trying so hard to reason, to stay. Emma had her difficulties too, struggling to fully forgave herself. It was painfully difficult to watch her try to prove herself innocent
and not questioning her own integrity. Now being a viewer, I do resonate and feel empathetic towards Emma. She proves to be a normal girl in a normal world. She was contended with her life, never really hurt anyone physically. Maybe her childhood was shaped with wounds of a kind but in the end she did choose not to act on her impulses. Overall Charlie's suspicion felt excessive as the incidents that happened in her life explains her fears but does not define who she became. Another beautiful perspective Emma brings to the movie with her character is that she seems super self composed. Even when things fall apart, she keeps coming back to it, trying to bring life with a newer perspective. I did enjoy the movie, it felt refreshing and new and short. so i rate it 8/10.
