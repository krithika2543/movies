1. <img width="312" height="312" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd5650b9-6ef0-422e-982b-1114dabcf8a4" />
The movie is about a deeply romantic couple who lived happily together, loved each other to the ends and had great chemistry. However their love story takes a unpleasant twist when they reveal their darkest secrets.
The lead Charlie falls into confusion where he finds hard to accept Emma, the love of his life for who she really was. He wanted to uncover more of what happened, which to my senses seems like his attempt to understand her. Emma as a growing teenager faced the scrutiny of bullyism and inability to fit in; had a really challenging childhood. It went to a level of severity where she had an intention of conducting a mass shooting in her school. Coming into such an awareness about Emma's past, Charlie finds himself broken into two halves where one half wants to run away from her; the psychopath while the other trying so hard to reason, to stay. Emma had her difficulties too, struggling to fully forgave herself. It was painfully difficult to watch her try to prove herself innocent and not questioning her own integrity. Now being a viewer, I do resonate and feel empathetic towards Emma. She proves to be a normal girl in a normal world. She was contended with her life, never really hurt anyone physically. Maybe her childhood was shaped with wounds of a kind but in the end she did choose not to act on her impulses. Overall Charlie's suspicion felt excessive as the incidents that happened in her life explains her fears but does not define who she became. Another beautiful perspective Emma brings to the movie with her character is that she seems super self composed. Even when things fall apart, she keeps coming back to it, trying to bring life with a newer perspective. I did enjoy the movie, it felt refreshing and new and short. so i rate it 8/10.

The story is about a doctor, a child psychologist. At the peak of his career, he discovers his success was built on a failure. When life presents him a second chance, to fix his mistakes, Dr. Malcom puts all his heart into it. Cole a 9year old boy suffered through his parents divorce. What makes the situation particularly interesting is the striking similarity between Cole and Malcom's former patient. It gives Malcom an opportunity to confront his failure he has been carrying with him. Despite all of this being true, the doctor also suffers through a bad marriage phase where he is not able to keep his wife happy. It was painful to watch Malcom so fixated on saving his patient but also being completely unable to salvage his own crumbling marriage. Nevertheless, Malcom understands the boy's struggle and helps him overcome his fears by being physically present with him. The devastating twist that Malcom himself is unaware that his encounters exist only because he is already dead. The doctor was also one of the creations of the boys ability to see ghosts or dead people who want another chance to finish their business before they finally leave. Overall I truly enjoyed how slow paced and attention to detail the movie was. so i rate it 8/10

The movie sets an example of a perfect feel good. What I genuinely loved about this movie is the simplicity itself. The story stars two important leads. Justin, a bachelor in his thirties. Loved by all but when it comes to his own romantic life, he chooses not to marry. On the other hand there is Ashley, a college student, struggling her way through family emotions and thriving in a new town. Her father being an unreliable alcoholic, causing her mom to take up all responsibilities. She had to leave behind her childhood home, friends, band and comfort due to financial strains that her family goes through. But meeting Justin, makes things change slowly for Ashley. Even though they seem like complete opposite poles, Justin's effortless charm, free people around him close to him which naturally attracted Ashley. What I genuinely loved about the movie is that feeling of innocent love. The way Ashley shows her affection to Justin while keeping it in secrecy from her family and friends. In this era of modern love being displayed as materialistic and flashy, the absence of grand gestures makes it a soothing watch and nostalgic. This magic is also seen in the way the people of the town always rooting and showing up for people they love. From planning trips together, to easily welcoming people, making them feel a part makes it completely wholesome. Despite all the struggles and her family completely opposing her love for Justin, Ashely tried to remain calm while deeply carrying the pain of separation. However the movie took a empowering turn when Ashley comes back as a grown up women, working her way through in a new country, she decides to finally stand up for herslf. She confesses her love to Justin again and actually fought for it. She turns out to be successful:))
